Our Roots

We grew up in the kitchen.

The Daily Beet opened on Girod Street in 2017 with one idea: New Orleans deserved real, honest food that happens to be good for you. We partner with local farms so the produce in your bowl travels miles, not months — flavor first, always plant-forward.
